This bibliography records the external resources identified for FLEXID. It distinguishes teaching and examination materials from the official case-law reference. It is a source-level bibliography, not a one-to-one mapping from resources to FLEXID instance IDs.

## Official case-law source

**Conseil d'État, decision no. 362444, 31 January 2014 — ArianeWeb analysis.**

Source : site internet du Conseil d'État.

https://www.conseil-etat.fr/fr/arianeweb/CE/analyse/2014-01-31/362444

This reference concerns the legitimate interest required for a change of surname under article 61 of the Civil Code. It is classified as legal-source material, separately from teaching exercises.
